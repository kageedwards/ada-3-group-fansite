Fun Facts

  1. A Brand-New Generation

    My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic premiered on The Hub on October 10, 2010, developed by animator Lauren Faust.

  2. Star Student

    Twilight began the series as Princess Celestia's personal student, sent to Ponyville to learn about friendship.

  3. Her Number One Assistant

    Spike, a baby dragon, is Twilight's assistant. He can send letters to Celestia by breathing magic fire on them.

  4. The Element of Magic

    Of the six Elements of Harmony, Twilight represents Magic, the spark that brings the other five together.

  5. Cutie Mark

    Her cutie mark is a large magenta six-pointed star surrounded by five small white stars.

  6. Earning Her Wings

    Twilight started as a unicorn and became an alicorn princess in the Season 3 finale, "Magical Mystery Cure."

  7. Family Ties

    Her big brother is Shining Armor, who married Princess Cadance, Twilight's old foalsitter.

  8. The Voice of Twilight

    Tara Strong voices Twilight, while Rebecca Shoichet provides her singing voice.
