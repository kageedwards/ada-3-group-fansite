Welcome to our Twilight fansite!

(no, not the book series)

Twilight Sparkle is the protagonist in the show My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic. She's a studious and compassionate pony.

As a child, Twilight attended the School for Gifted Unicorns, where she struggle for a time with magic. She persevered through her studies, and her talents were noticed by Princess Celestia, who took her in as an apprentice.

Celestia grew concerned that Twilight was stuck in the books to the point that it was affecting her social life. She decided to send her student away to a small town named Ponyville to become the new librarian, in hopes that she'd make some friends.

The show follows Twilight and her five closest friends as they face numerous adventures throughout Equestria. Relying on one another, they soon realize that their individual strengths not only complement one another but coincide with the Elements of Harmony, relics which represent the six core pillars of a stable world.

As she learns and grows, she eventually transforms into an Alicorn as a result of the enlightenment she experiences from the lessons learned with her friends.

Celestia gives Twilight a more central role in the royal house and state affairs as she gets older and begins exemplifying true leadership.
